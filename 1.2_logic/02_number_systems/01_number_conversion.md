To convert 156 decimal (base 10) to octal (base 8):

### Step 1: Divide by 8

$$
156\div8=19\text{ remainder }4
$$

$$
19\div8=2\text{ remainder }3
$$

$$
2\div8=0\text{ remainder }2
$$

### Step 2: Read the remainders from bottom to top

The remainders are 2, 3, 4.

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{156_{10}=234_8}
$$

Check:

$$
2\times8^2+3\times8^1+4\times8^0
$$

$$
=128+24+4=\boxed{156}
$$

✅ Final answer: $234_8$
